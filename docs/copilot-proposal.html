Step 2 · Approval model

One state machine for every stage, scene and asset

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> NotStarted
  NotStarted --> Working: Co-Pilot starts the task
  Working --> AIReview: output ready
  AIReview --> Working: safe fix, within retry limit
  AIReview --> NeedsOwner: gate reached, or issue AI cannot fix
  AIReview --> AIReviewed: passed (scenes in Co-Pilot)
  AIReviewed --> NeedsOwner: shown at the next gate
  NeedsOwner --> OwnerApproved: you approve
  NeedsOwner --> ChangesRequested: you request changes
  ChangesRequested --> Working
  Working --> Blocked: rights, facts, retries used up
  Blocked --> Working: you decide
  OwnerApproved --> Outdated: upstream content changed
  Outdated --> Working: redo (old approval stays in use)
  OwnerApproved --> Completed: final export

Every approval records

  • Project, item and version id
  • Approver (owner, or AI reviewer for AI-reviewed)
  • Timestamp
  • Hash of the approved content
  • Hash of the settings it depended on (voice, style, dictionary)

Invalidation

  • Each item lists the upstream hashes it was made from.
  • When one changes, only items built from it become Outdated.
  • One narration line → its take, its scene, the film, the paid plan. Other scenes untouched.
  • No approval carries over to changed content.

Two kinds of "good"

  • AI reviewed passed the technical and independent art review.
  • Owner approved only you, at a gate.
  • The UI never shows the first as the second.

Orchestrator

A deterministic step runner. It reads the project's events, finds the next pending task, runs it as a tracked job, records the result, and stops at gates and exceptions. It holds no state of its own, so a restart simply reads the events again.

AI worker

Each task is a written brief with inputs and a strict answer format. The answer is validated, stored as a proposal by "ai:writer" or "ai:reviewer", and linked to its evidence. Reviewers run as separate invocations that never see the writer's reasoning.

What stays the same

The Studio store, event log, take store, art builders, engine, reviewer, gate, gateway and ledger. The dashboard keeps working on the same data. Existing projects keep their mode and approvals.

  • You change the visual style
  • A scene fails the independent review
  • A scene lacks licensed assets

  • Server killed during a render
  • Approval outdated by an upstream change
  • Switch from Co-Pilot to Manual
  • Unauthorized and over-budget paid calls refused
  • Finished draft played and reviewed on a phone

  • Blank opening frame, silent tail
  • Stale blockers
  • Phone overflow
  • Decision sync
  • Paid-provider access

Open decisions

Six answers needed before step 3

1 · Who is "the AI" between gates

Recommended: the Studio runs Claude Code headless as its worker on the machine that runs the Studio (your Mac, or this development container). No API key in the app and no per-use billing. Alternative: a direct model API, which is a paid provider and would need its own budget and authorization like ElevenLabs.

2 · Research and image sources

Web research with sources recorded per claim; images only from sources with licence data (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress, Europeana) or your uploads. Commercial and brand images only with a licence you provide.

3 · Styles to make renderable

Paper Story, technical drawing, kinetic type, maps and timelines work today. Japanese-inspired illustration and watercolour need new free renderers. Cinematic documentary and archival collage need licensed photos or footage. Which to build now?

4 · Draft voice speed and pauses

gTTS has neither. They would be audio processing after the voice is made, labelled that way. Acceptable?

5 · Retry limit

Two automatic correction rounds per scene before Co-Pilot stops and asks you. Change it?

6 · Gate 5 strictness

Paid narration needs Gate 5 approved and no open critical review findings. Minor findings may stay open with your note. Agree?
