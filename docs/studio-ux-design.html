W12 Going back. Before any upstream change: exactly what becomes outdated, and what stays.

Approval and revision rules

Nothing approved is ever silently changed or lost

Draft→Ready for review→Approved (locked)→OutdatedorReplaced by vN+1·Rejected

Versions

  • An approved item never changes. A change makes a new version.
  • The approved version stays in use until you approve the new one, which replaces it in one step.
  • Rejected and replaced versions stay in history.

Gates

  • A step opens only when the step before it is approved and nothing upstream is outdated.
  • Scenes are approved in order. Later scenes can be previewed, not approved.
  • Approval needs evidence: audio heard, scene played, film watched with sound.

Going back

  • You can reopen any step at any time.
  • Before saving, an impact sheet lists what becomes outdated and what is unaffected.
  • Outdated items keep their approved version, marked, until redone and re-approved.

Paid and brand

  • Paid narration only after G7, with an exact plan, cost, typed confirmation and budget. One run per authorization. No automatic retries.
  • Time Keeper and Diabetes History styles, voices and assets never mix.

Open decisions

Five answers needed before S1

1 · Who researches and writes

Today Claude does it as the operator. Option A: the Studio queues a request and a Claude session answers (no extra cost, needs a session). Option B: the Studio calls a model API itself (per-use cost, a key in the app). Recommended: A.

2 · Image sources

Free sources with licence data (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress, Europeana) plus your uploads. Commercial archives and brand press images only with a licence you provide.

3 · New styles

Watercolour and Japanese-inspired illustration need new free renderers (S4, larger). Paid AI concepts stay locked. Which, if any, to build?

4 · Draft voice controls

gTTS offers no speed or pause control. Speed and pauses would be audio processing after synthesis, labelled as such. Acceptable?

5 · Scene order

Strictly in order, as you asked: later scenes can be previewed and prepared, not approved. Confirm.
