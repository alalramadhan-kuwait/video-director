# Motion graphics — the options

Everything is drawn by `vendor/paper-story` (DOM + headless Chromium + ffmpeg): nothing is generated by a model, so a
frame can be previewed in seconds and a render is repeatable. Element reference: `vendor/paper-story/references/elements.md`;
taste and pacing: `references/style-guide.md` and `references/production.md` there; hooks: `references/hooks.md`.

## The four looks (offer 2–3, each with a real frame of THIS film)
| look (id) | in Arabic | feels | best for | needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `paper-story` | قصة ورقية وثائقية | warm paper cut-outs, layers, slow camera | stories of people and brands (Royal Oak) | nothing extra |
| `technical` | رسم تقني توضيحي | pencil on light paper, labelled parts | how a watch works (crown, case, movement) | parts to label |
| `ink-keynote` | عرض داكن | dark ink, big type, strong contrast, luxury | short punchy facts, launches | short lines |
| `archive` | كولاج أرشيفي | real licensed photos and documents on paper | history with real people/watches | licensed photos for most scenes |

Rule: **a real photograph first** (`tools/find_photo.py`), a drawing only when none is found. A look is a preference
order of techniques (`art/styles.yaml`); each scene uses the first technique it has material for.

## Scene techniques (`director/studio/art.py`, `scene_visual(technique, params, theme, dur, banner)`)
| technique | what it shows | main params |
|---|---|---|
| `kinetic_type` | big words that rise in, optional red stamp | `lines[{text,size,at}]`, `stamp{text}` |
| `animated_timeline` | years on a drawn line, one card each | `events[{year,label,hero}]` |
| `map` | a map that moves, pins and routes | `view`, `pins`, `routes`, `labels` |
| `technical_illustration` | a watch part drawn and labelled (crown, case, water) | `kind`, `parts`, `card` |
| `paper_cut` | layered paper scenery (sea, cliffs), counter | `cliffs`, `counter`, `push` |
| `parallax_25d` | 2.5D depth push on paper layers | `push` |
| `archival_collage` | real photos as taped prints, author credit on screen | `photos[{asset,desc,aspect}]`, `tags` |
| `product_macro` | one product photo full-frame, slow zoom | `asset`, `desc` |
| `newspaper_card` | a newspaper front page card with a stamp | `header`, `title`, `body`, `stamp` |
| `swiss_grid` | clean grid and type — experimental, not picked for a film | as `kinetic_type` |
| `dark_keynote` | kinetic type on black (Opuscar-inspired) | as `kinetic_type` |
| `product_reveal` | a real watch photo revealed under a spotlight, name and year | `asset`, `desc`, `name`, `year`, `credit` |
| `spec_card` | specifications, or two watches compared row by row (sourced figures only) | `title`, `columns`, `rows[{label,values}]`, `source` |
| `quote_card` | a quotation, who said it, where it was printed | `quote`, `who`, `source` |
| `archive_photo` | a historical photograph: a tall one fills the frame and the camera drifts, a wide one is a large print; a when-and-where card and the credit. A portrait has the person's name in the banner or card | `asset`, `desc`, `card`, `move` (in/out/left/right), `pos`, `print: true` (keep a tall photo whole) |
| `blueprint` | the opening: a real drawing from the archive (a patent, a plan) on blueprint paper, revealed by a scanning light, a cold glow, hook words, a stamp, a card naming the drawing; never an invented cartoon | `asset`, `lines`, `stamp`, `card`, `reveal`, `glow_at` |
| `city_zoom` | the map flies from the country into a city, then its colours and landmarks rise like a pop-up book, then its name | `city` (an id in `director/studio/cities.py`), `sub` |
| `paper_cutout` | the whole watch, instrument or person cut out of its photo onto the scene's paper (paper edge, shadow), or drawn in the same paper style when no photo shows it whole | `asset` (a photo slot) and/or `drawn: {kind}` (`radiomir`, `luminor`, `oyster`, `round`; with `person: true`: `man_suit`, `woman_dress`, `worker`, `diver`, `scientist`), `card`, `tilt` |
| `watch_movement` | how a mechanical watch works: the movement drawn in 3D and running (barrel and mainspring, gear train at real ratios, escape wheel stepping, pallet fork, balance and hairspring), then lifting apart in layers with Arabic names; always labelled a simplified principle | `explode_at`, `title`, `parts` (which to name), `spin` |
| `stat_counter` | one sourced number on rolling digits that lands with a small burst on the spoken number | `value`, `prefix`/`suffix` (an Arabic unit goes on the left), `label`, `land`, `source` |
| `part_of_whole` | «X of every N»: N small figures fill in, X light up in gold | `total`, `hot`, `cols`, `land`, `title`, `source` |
| `trend_chart` | a line that draws itself through sourced values | `values`, `from_label`, `to_label`, `title`, `source` |
| `compare_bars` | two to four bars with their values and units, one in gold | `items[{label,value,unit,hot}]`, `title`, `source` |

The four number templates are drawn by `vendor/motion-kit` (video-ad-editor's `motion-kit.js`, MIT, by Majed
Alzaabi) through the engine's `kit` element (`vendor/motion-kit/kit-element.js`): a full-frame canvas in 1080×1920
design pixels, each component a pure function of the scene time. Its other components (`statCard`, `statGrid`,
`kinetic`, `timeline`, `stamp`, `techFrame`, `burst`, `shake`) can be called from a scene the same way:
`{"type": "kit", "draw": [{"fn": "statCard", "s": 0.4, "box": {...}, ...}]}`.

**Story worlds and moods** (on any template, from the film file): `set:` draws the world behind the scene with its
ambience — `underwater`, `sea_night`, `florence`, `workshop`, `radium`, `archive` (`director/studio/sets.py`); a map
or a full-frame photo gets only its sound. `mood:` adds a light grade under the words and a sound — `mystery`, `fear`,
`sadness`, `tension`, `joy`, `wonder` (`director/studio/moods.py`). Maps use the colour atlas look by default.
A city that is not in `cities.py` yet: add it (position, country as the map names it, palette, sound, three skyline
layers of its landmarks), then render its arrival and look at it.

**The catalogue** — `art/catalogue.yaml` is the list of every template with its purpose and status (implemented /
experimental / unavailable); only implemented ones are picked for a film. See them all, drawn, with the layout check:
`python3 tools/motion_catalogue.py <dir> --photo <a licensed photo> [--video]` (opens as `<dir>/catalogue.html`).

Themes: `tk-bone` (warm off-white), `tk-ink` / `tk-black` (dark), `tk-graphite`. Time Keeper boxes are #111111 / #FFFFFF;
no accent colour is invented. Every Time Keeper film ends on the Time Keeper end card (`vendor/paper-story/tk_endcard.py`).

## Making a scene and checking it
```python
from director.studio import art, engine
sc = art.scene_visual('kinetic_type', {'lines': [{'text': 'أول ساعة مقاومة للماء', 'size': 80}]}, 'tk-bone', 4.0, banner='أويستر ١٩٢٦')
sc.update(dur=4.0, voice='voice/l1.wav', voiceAt=0.3, captions='…')
sb = {'width': 720, 'height': 1280, 'fps': 30, 'dir': 'rtl', **art.style('timekeeper'), 'scenes': [sc]}
engine.check(sb, workdir)                       # layout faults: text cut by the frame, prints over prints, things under captions
engine.stills(sb, workdir, [2.0], scale=0.5)    # look at the frame yourself before rendering
engine.video(path_to_json, out_mp4, scale=1.5, extra=('--crf', 24))   # 1080×1920, ≈ 20–26 MB for 2½ minutes
```
Keep content between y≈380 and y≈980 (the banner is on top, captions at the bottom). One idea per scene, 2–8 s.
Look at every still: a frame you have not seen is not checked.
